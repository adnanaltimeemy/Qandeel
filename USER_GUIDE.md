# Qandeel User Guide

**Qandeel – قنديل · Your Faith. Your Life. Your Light.**

Qandeel brings prayer times, Qibla, the Quran, duas, the Islamic calendar and your daily trackers together in one calm, private app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It is free, with no ads and no account.

> **A note on religious content.** Qandeel shows the Quran, hadith and duas with their references, and points out where scholars differ. It does not issue rulings (fatwas). For personal religious questions, please ask a qualified scholar or your local imam.

## 1. Getting started

When you first open Qandeel, a short welcome guides you through:

1. **Your name (optional).** It's used for a friendly greeting and stays on your device.
2. **Your location.** Tap **Use my location**, or **Choose a city instead**. Qandeel needs a location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla.
3. **Calculation method.** Qandeel suggests one for your region. You can change it later. See [Prayer times](#3-prayer-times).
4. **Notifications.** Allow notifications if you'd like prayer reminders.

You can change all of these later in **Profile ▸ Settings**.

**Finding your way around**
- **iPhone:** use the tabs along the bottom: Home, Prayer, Quran, Duas and Profile. Qibla, Tasbih, the Calendar, Good deeds, Prayer guides and the Journal are on the Home screen's **Quick access** tiles. Profile holds everything else.
- **iPad:** every section is listed in the sidebar on the left.

## 2. Home

Home shows your day at a glance:

- **Next prayer:** its name and time, with a live countdown.
- **Today's prayers:** tap a prayer to mark it as **Prayed**, **Prayed late** or **Missed**.
- **In Ramadan:** today's suhoor and iftar times.
- **Verse, dua and hadith of the day.** Each comes with its reference. Hadith include the original Arabic narration; tap to show it.
- **Ask Qandeel:** a quick way to search the app's content. See [Ask Qandeel](#12-ask-noor).
- **Quick access:** tiles for Qibla, Tasbih, Calendar, Good deeds, Prayer guides, Journal and Continue reading.

The greeting card changes through the day, with gentle reminders for morning and evening remembrance (adhkar), Jumu'ah and before sleeping.

---

## 3. Prayer times

Open the **Prayer** tab to see today's times for Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. Tap **Monthly timetable** to see the whole month; the share button sends it as a spreadsheet (CSV) file. **Prayer tracker** is also here.

### Choosing your settings

Go to **Profile ▸ Settings ▸ Prayer calculation**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Calculation method** | The sun angles used for Fajr and Isha. Choose the one your community or mosque follows, for example Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, Egyptian, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore or Tehran. Each option explains its angles. |
| **Asr** | **Standard** (Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali) or **Hanafi** (later Asr). |
| **High-latitude rule** | In the UK and other northern places, the sky may not get fully dark in summer. This rule decides how Fajr and Isha are worked out then. If your times look unusual between May and July, try a different rule or follow your mosque's timetable. |
| **Adjustments** | Add or subtract minutes for each prayer, to match a timetable you trust. |

> **Why might Qandeel differ from my mosque by a few minutes?** Mosques use different methods, add safety margins, or follow local moon and dawn sightings. If you pray with a particular mosque, the most accurate option is to use its timetable. See [Match my mosque](#4-match-my-mosque).

## 4. Match my mosque

Use your mosque's own timetable, including **iqamah** (congregation) and **Jumu'ah** times.

Go to **Profile ▸ Match my mosque**:

1. **Prayer times from:** choose **Calculated** or **My mosque's timetable**.
2. **Import timetable:** pick a CSV file from your mosque, from Files, email or a download. Qandeel understands common column names and UK-style 12-hour times such as "1:15".
3. **No CSV?** Share the blank **template** and send the blank template to your mosque. They fill it in with Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, then send it back.
4. **Iqamah:** for days without an iqamah time, you can set a default such as "15 minutes after the start time". You can also turn on **Remind me 10 minutes before iqamah**.
5. **Jumu'ah:** add one or more Jumu'ah times and choose **Remind me an hour before Jumu'ah**.
6. **Match calculated times to this mosque:** this sets your minute adjustments so that calculated times stay close to the mosque's, even after the imported timetable runs out.

Your widgets, Apple Watch and notifications all use the mosque's times once it's selected. To stop using it, tap **Remove timetable**.

## 5. Notifications and adhan

Go to **Profile ▸ Settings ▸ Prayer notifications**.

- **Per-prayer style:** choose **Off**, **Silent banner**, **Notification sound** or **Adhan** for each prayer.
- **Reminder before each prayer:** a heads-up 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes before.
- **Suhoor alarm in Ramadan:** 15 to 90 minutes before Fajr.
- **Jumu'ah reminder** on Fridays.
- **Next prayer on the Lock Screen:** a live countdown. See [section 14](#14-widgets-lock-screen-and-dynamic-island).

### Using your own adhan

Go to **Settings ▸ Adhan sound** to import an adhan recording you have permission to use, for example from your mosque. You can choose a separate recording for Fajr. iOS limits notification sounds to 30 seconds, so Qandeel trims the recording automatically. Then set the prayers you want to **Adhan**.

### Keeping reminders reliable

iOS allows an app to schedule only a limited number of notifications in advance. Qandeel plans about **two weeks ahead** and tops them up in the background. If you haven't opened Qandeel for a long while, it will remind you to open it so it can schedule the next batch.

Prayer alerts are marked **Time Sensitive**, so they can break through a Focus mode. If you don't want this, adjust it in *iOS Settings ▸ Focus*.

## 6. Qibla

Open **Qibla** (from Home's Quick access, or the sidebar on iPad) and hold your device flat. Turn until the arrow points towards the Kaaba; the phone gives a gentle tap when you're facing the Qibla. The screen also shows how far away Makkah is.

**Tips for accuracy**
- Stand away from metal, magnets, cars and speakers.
- If Qandeel says compass accuracy is low, move the device in a figure-of-eight to calibrate.
- The direction is calculated from your location, so make sure your location is correct.

---

## 7. Prayer tracker

Open **Prayer tracker** (in the Prayer tab, or tap *Today's prayers* on Home) to record each prayer as **Prayed**, **Prayed late** or **Missed**. You can see **this week** at a glance and keep a private count of **make-up (qada)** prayers, reducing it as you complete them. It's a tool to help you stay consistent, not a score.

You can also tick off prayers from the **interactive widget** on your Home Screen or from your **Apple Watch**.

To start again, go to **Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Erase my prayer and fasting records**.

## 8. Quran

The full Arabic text (Uthmani script), with the **Saheeh International** (English) and **Maududi** (Urdu) translations, is built in and works **offline**.

### Reading
- Browse by **surah** or **juz'**. Qandeel remembers where you stopped; tap **Continue reading** to return.
- Touch and hold a verse to **bookmark**, **share** or **copy** it.
- Open the reader settings to choose the Arabic font (KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs or Amiri Quran), the Arabic and translation text sizes, the translation, whether to show it, and the reciter.

### Listening
- Choose a **reciter** in *Settings ▸ Quran*. Options include Alafasy, as-Sudais, Al-Husary, al-Minshawi, Abdul Basit, Al Muaiqly and Ash-Shaatree.
- Press **Play**. The verse being recited is highlighted, and you can control playback from the Lock Screen.
- **Download for offline:** in a surah, open the **⋯** menu and choose **Download recitation for offline**. Manage storage in *Settings ▸ Quran*.

### Learning
- **Word by word:** shows the meaning under each Arabic word. This needs an internet connection the first time.
- **Memorisation mode:** hides the verses so you can recite from memory. Tap a verse to reveal it.
- **Khatm planner** (in *Profile ▸ Reading*, or *Calendar ▸ Reading*): choose a finish date, and Qandeel splits the Quran into equal daily portions.

### More translations
English (Pickthall, Yusuf Ali), Urdu (Jalandhry), French, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay, Bengali and Spanish translations download when you first choose them.

## 9. Duas and tasbih

**Duas** are grouped by occasion: morning and evening, prayer, food, travel, sleep, distress, family and more. Each dua shows the **Arabic**, the **transliteration**, the **translation** and its **source**. Tap the heart to add it to **Favourites**, or share it.

**Tasbih** is a digital counter. Tap to count; you feel a light tap on each count and a stronger one when you reach your target. After prayer, the tasbih of 33 × SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah and Allahu Akbar moves to the next phrase for you. You can also add your own dhikr.

## 10. Islamic calendar, fasting, reading and Eid

Open **Calendar** from Home's Quick access, or the sidebar on iPad.

- **Hijri calendar:** today's Hijri date, plus upcoming events such as Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Day of Arafah, Eid al-Adha, Islamic New Year and Ashura.
  - **Hijri date adjustment** (*Settings ▸ Islamic calendar*): if your community's moon sighting differs from the Umm al-Qura calendar, shift dates by up to ±2 days.
- **Fasting:** track Ramadan fasts, voluntary fasts (Mondays and Thursdays, the White Days, Arafah, Ashura, six days of Shawwal) and make-up fasts. The **Fasting guide & recommended fasts** covers why we fast, Ramadan essentials, who is excused and the days not to fast, with references.
- **Reading:** add the books you're reading, log your progress, and follow your **Khatm plan** for the Quran.
- **Eid:** the **Eid guide & checklist** covers Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha: before Eid, Eid morning, the day itself, the first ten days of Dhu al-Hijjah, Udhiyah (Qurbani) and the Days of Tashreeq, with a checklist to tick off.

## 11. Ramadan

- **Suhoor and iftar times** appear on Home throughout Ramadan. With *Next prayer on the Lock Screen* turned on, you get a countdown to iftar.
- **Suhoor alarm:** see [Notifications](#5-notifications-and-adhan).
- **Taraweeh tracker** (*Profile ▸ Ramadan*): record each night's rak'ahs and whether you prayed at the mosque or at home. The odd nights of the last ten are marked.
- **My Ramadan summary:** a card showing your fasts, taraweeh nights and juz' of Quran read. Only you decide whether to share it.

## 12. Ask Qandeel

**Ask Qandeel** helps you *find* what the built-in sources say. Examples:
- "duas for anxiety"
- "verses about patience"
- "how to perform wudu"

**How it works:** Ask Qandeel searches the Quran, the duas, the hadith and the guides **on your device**. It shows the matching passages **exactly as they appear in the source**, each with its reference.

**What it won't do**
- It never writes or paraphrases religious text.
- It doesn't give rulings. If you ask for one, for example "Is X halal?", it explains that this needs a scholar, and shows any related passages for you to read.
- It doesn't send your questions anywhere.

## 13. Guides, good deeds and journal

- **Wudu and prayer guides:** step-by-step guides, with references, to wudu, how to pray, the number of rak'ahs for each prayer, shortening and combining prayers when travelling, and the Janazah prayer. They note where the schools of thought differ.
- **Good deeds:** add the small deeds you want to keep up, for example charity or calling family, tick them off each day, and see your week. It's a private reminder, not a score.
- **Private journal:** write reflections and gratitude.
  - Turn on **Lock with Face ID / passcode** for extra privacy.
  - The journal is stored **only on this device** and is never synced.

## 14. Widgets, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

**Adding a widget:** touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap **Edit** (or **+**), then search for **Qandeel**.

- **Small:** the next prayer and its countdown.
- **Medium and large:** today's prayers, with **tick buttons**. Tap one to mark it as prayed without opening the app.
- **Lock Screen:** the next prayer, in circular, rectangular or inline styles.

**Live Activity:** turn on **Settings ▸ Prayer notifications ▸ Next prayer on the Lock Screen**. A live countdown then appears on your Lock Screen and, on supported iPhones, in the **Dynamic Island**. In Ramadan it counts down to iftar.

## 15. Apple Watch

Install Qandeel on your Watch from the **Watch** app on your iPhone (*My Watch ▸ Available Apps*).

- See today's prayers and the next prayer's countdown.
- Tap a prayer to mark it as prayed; your iPhone updates too.
- Add a **Qandeel complication** (circular, rectangular, inline or corner) to your watch face for the next prayer at a glance.

Your Watch gets its prayer times from your iPhone, so they always match, including your mosque's timetable. Open Qandeel on your iPhone now and then to keep the Watch up to date.

## 17. Sync, themes, language and accessibility

- **iCloud sync:** go to *Settings ▸ iPhone & iPad sync*. Your trackers, bookmarks and favourites follow you between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Your journal, location and settings stay on each device.
- **Colour theme:** go to *Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Colour theme* and choose Emerald, Midnight, Desert or Ocean. You can also choose **Automatic**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Language:** Qandeel is available in **English**, **العربية (Arabic)** and **اردو (Urdu)**. Arabic and Urdu use a right-to-left layout. To change Qandeel's language, go to *Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Language & region*, or *iOS Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Qandeel ▸ Language*.
- **Accessibility:**
  - Qandeel supports **Dynamic Type**, including the Arabic text.
  - Arabic is read by **VoiceOver** with an Arabic voice.
  - Prayers, trackers and controls have VoiceOver labels.

## 18. Troubleshooting

**My location shows the wrong city (for example London instead of Manchester).**
- Go to *Settings ▸ Location* and tap **Use my current location** again.
- Make sure location access is allowed in *iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services ▸ Qandeel* (**While Using the App**), and that **Precise Location** is on.
- If you're indoors, step near a window and wait a few seconds. Or search for your town by name.

**Notifications didn't arrive.**
- Check *iOS Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ Qandeel*: notifications should be allowed, and Time Sensitive turned on.
- Check that the prayer isn't set to **Off** in *Settings ▸ Prayer notifications*.
- Open Qandeel. It reschedules reminders every time it opens.
- Check whether a **Focus** mode is silencing Qandeel.

**My prayer times differ from my mosque's.**
- Check the **calculation method**, the **Asr** setting and the **high-latitude rule**.
- Or import your mosque's timetable. See [Match my mosque](#4-match-my-mosque).

**The Qibla direction looks wrong.**
- Move away from metal and electronics, and calibrate with a figure-of-eight motion.
- Check that your location is correct.

**A translation, recitation or word-by-word meanings won't load.**
These need an internet connection the first time. The built-in Arabic text and the Saheeh International and Maududi translations always work offline.

**The widget or Watch shows old times.**
Open Qandeel on your iPhone once. Widgets and the Watch refresh from the app.
